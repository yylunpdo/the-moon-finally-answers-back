An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **The Moon Finally Answers Back** — one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> You wished on me, howled at me, and promised me you'd change at 2 a.m. Four billion years of night shifts — and not one of you has ever wished me goodnight back.

Four billion years of listening, and the Moon's one complaint is the politeness gap: humanity takes wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises up to the sky, and never once returns the goodnight.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, fully deterministic) writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it twice and the output is byte-identical every time:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Entry

- Program: `main.py`
- Output: `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 161 characters, no line breaks
